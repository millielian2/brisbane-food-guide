01 / THE CITY

BRISBANE
THROUGH EXPLORING.

Brisbane is a city shaped by its river, its neighbourhoods and the people who call it home. From busy city streets to quiet parks, local restaurants and hidden corners, there is always somewhere new to explore.

This project is a personal collection of places discovered around Brisbane. Rather than trying to list everything the city has to offer, the goal is to document places that were memorable, interesting or simply worth stopping for.

Places around Brisbane worth exploring, visiting and remembering.

Brisbane Sign / South Bank

📍 South Bank Parklands, South Brisbane QLD 4101

Iconic giant colourful BRISBANE letters right by the river — one of the most photographed spots in the city. Surrounded by riverside walkways, parklands and stunning skyline views.

Robinson Park

📍 221 Fairfield Rd, Fairfield QLD 4103

A lovely local park with plenty of green space, mature trees and peaceful surroundings. Great for a stroll, relaxing break or picnic while exploring the area.
