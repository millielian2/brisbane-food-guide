01 / THE CITY

BRISBANE
THROUGH FOOD.

Brisbane is a city shaped by its river, its neighbourhoods and the people who call it home. Its food scene reflects that mix — from busy city streets to small local bakeries and restaurants tucked into suburban corners.

This project is a personal collection of places discovered around Brisbane over the past two months. Rather than trying to list every restaurant in the city, the goal is to document places that were memorable, interesting or simply worth stopping for.

Places to Visit

Brisbane Sign / South Bank

📍 South Bank Parklands, South Brisbane QLD 4101

Iconic giant colourful BRISBANE letters right by the river — one of the most photographed spots in the city. Surrounded by riverside walkways, parklands, and stunning skyline views.

Robinson Park

📍 221 Fairfield Rd, Fairfield QLD 4103

A lovely local park with plenty of green space, mature trees, and peaceful surroundings. Great for a stroll, relaxing break, or picnic while exploring the area.
